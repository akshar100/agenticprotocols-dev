Visa Launches 'Intelligent Commerce Connect' for AI Agentic Payments

Visa has announced Intelligent Commerce Connect, a new platform designed to let AI agents autonomously browse, select, and pay for goods and services on behalf of consumers. Serving as a network-, protocol-, and token vault-agnostic entry point, it marks a significant step forward in enabling agentic commerce for merchants and agent builders.
